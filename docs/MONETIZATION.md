# Monetization landscape (researched 2026-11)

Scope decided: **generally-SFW persona on Instagram**, funneling off-platform.
This file is the reference for where money actually flows.

## Instagram itself

No meaningful direct revenue for this account type — Reels Play bonuses are
dead, Subscriptions require real-creator history. IG is purely the funnel +
brand-deal surface. Sponsored posts for a persona at scale run roughly
€1k/post (Aitana-level). Instagram removes AI-generated nudity and requires
AI labels on realistic generated media.

## The funnel targets

| Platform | Creator keeps | AI persona? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fanvue | 80% | **Yes** — openly welcomed; disclosure + KYC'd operator required. Ran the "Miss AI" contest; AI was ~15% of platform revenue in 2023 |
| OnlyFans | 80% | No — bars wholly AI-generated accounts; AI content must be the verified human creator's own likeness, labeled #ai |
| Fansly | 80% | Non-photorealistic only; photorealistic AI banned even labeled |
| ManyVids | 60% vids / 80% most else | No new AI-only accounts; >3 AI vids on a human account risks action |
| Patreon | ~90% | Illustrated/animated adult AI only; no hyperrealistic |
| X (Twitter) | subs @ high thresholds | Allowed behind content warning; useful extra top-of-funnel |

Fanvue is the natural fit for a disclosed AI persona. Since our persona is
SFW-only, also keep mainstream options open (brand deals, affiliate,
page-driven products) — an SFW persona is not locked into adult platforms.

## Reference earnings

- **Aitana López** (The Clueless, Barcelona): ~€3k/mo average, up to
  €10k/mo, after ~1.5 years — 400k+ IG followers, €15/mo Fanvue with ~4k
  subs, plus brand deals. Labeled "perfil generado con IA".
- Median AI influencer: ~$0. Generation is the easy 20%; persona
  believability + consistent posting + growth is the business.
- Adult tube RPM for comparison (rejected path): ~$0.69/1k Pornhub,
  $0.23–0.59/1k xHamster, sub-$1 XVideos — and all require verified
  uploaders with per-performer consent docs, which kills any
  reupload-compilation model.

## Practical shape

`SFW IG feed (3-7 posts/wk) -> link-in-bio -> Fanvue (labeled AI) + brand
deals`. Feed content stays inside Meta's rules; anything spicier lives
behind the paid platform's own gates.
